# Automated MOTM Formatter

## What Problem it Solves:
Time Loss: The MOTM Formatter automatically organizes and formats draft meeting notes, reducing the amount of time secretaeial staff spend manually cleaning and styling documents.

Formatting Inconsistency: The program applies a standardized format to every set of meeting notes, ensuring that documents remain consistent regardless of the meeting or note-taker.

Buried Deliverables: The formatter clearly organizes action items, assigned owners, and deadlines into structure sections, making important deliverables easier to identify and track.

## How To Run:
Step 1: Fill up the details about what happened during the meeting.
Step 2: Click the "Generate PDF" button at the end of the form.
Step 3: Check your downloads file and you will officially see the automated MOTM
